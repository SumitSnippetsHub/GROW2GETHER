# Competitor notes

Taken from public homepage copy in November 2026. This is not a ranking study and not a traffic estimate. Volumes are unverified.

## What their homepages emphasise

- **Masamind** (masamind.com) calls itself a leading website design agency in Delhi NCR. The homepage lists business and corporate sites, WordPress, e-commerce, Shopify, redesign, maintenance, and UI/UX. The promise is growth and SEO-friendly sites.
- **NJ Technos** (njtechnos.com) says it was established in 2006 and has been building sites for over 19 years. Services named: custom website design, e-commerce, responsive design, CMS, maintenance. The homepage talks about leads and sales.
- **CITEC** (citec.in) says more than 15 years, and lists website design, CMS, portals, e-commerce, WordPress, Shopify, OpenCart, and SEO, covering Delhi and Noida.
- **SAM Web Studio** (samwebstudio.com) says it has worked since 2012 and names “more than 320 brands”. The homepage covers websites, apps, software, e-commerce, redesign, and a large SEO and ads menu.
- **Webror** (webror.com) is closer in shape: a Delhi studio, Next.js and React named, Shopify and WordPress, automation, and SEO built into the site. It also promises Core Web Vitals “in the green” and chatbots.

## What grow2gether should not copy

- “Leading”, year-counts, and client totals that this studio has not published.
- A long menu of SEO, ads, OpenCart and marketplaces this studio does not sell.
- Outcome lines (rankings, revenue, “10X”) that are not measured here.

## Where the pages can compete

- A named founder, a written scope, and a published floor of ₹5,900.
- Separate URLs for website, design, shop, Shopify, web app, Next.js, redesign, maintenance, automation and UI, each with its own intent.
- Case studies that say who did the work (freelance, independent during employment, or team) instead of a logo wall.
- A Delhi page that says New Delhi, remote calls, and no walk-in office.

## Google Business Profile checklist

Do this in the Google account, not in the codebase.

1. Create or claim the profile for grow2gether in New Delhi.
2. Category: website designer, or the closest match Google offers. Do not add categories for services you do not sell.
3. Service area: Delhi NCR. Do not add a street address unless there is a public office clients can visit. There is not one on the site.
4. Website: `https://www.grow2gether.in`.
5. Description: founder-led studio, custom websites, shops and apps, fixed price after a free 30-minute call. No invented project counts.
6. Phone and email: use the public contact path. Do not put the private booking inbox on the profile.
7. Photos: the logo and real project screenshots only.
8. After a finished project, ask the client if they will leave a review. Suggested message: “If the site is doing what we scoped, a short Google review of the work with grow2gether helps the next person decide. Only if you are happy to.”
